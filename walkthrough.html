4. Audit Trail & The "Wells Fargo Problem" (1:35 - 1:55)

Every decision, registration, and revocation event is logged with exact ISO timestamps in gatekeeper.db.

Why this matters: In the Wells Fargo unauthorized accounts scandal, actions were taken without clear proof of customer consent. If an autonomous agent buys products after a customer thought they cancelled it, who pays?

With our immutable timestamped table, Amazon and the customer have unalterable proof of whether the agent was valid or revoked at the exact second the request arrived.
